## Confidence Caps

- Metadata-only/unverified evidence cannot yield High confidence.
- Indirect-only evidence cannot exceed Low.
- An unresolved direct conflict cannot exceed Moderate.
- High confidence for mechanism, comparative, generalization, persistence, or system-value claims normally requires at least two independent direct, verified, full-context sources.
- A narrow existence claim may reach High with one method-valid direct source if the allowed wording remains equally narrow.
