## Portable checks

The collection maintenance command validates the package and runs its applicable
offline regressions. Generated scaffolds are test inputs, not completed research.
Use this Skill's validator and schema for an explicitly requested run. A structural
pass does not establish scientific truth or reader comprehension.

## Historical regression

Historical studies and maintenance evidence remain outside the public repository.
Full Mapper's historical regression accepts an explicit MAPPER_LEGACY_WORKSPACE.
It checks the recorded validation result and before/after file hashes without
rewriting the old runs. Public CI uses synthetic fixtures; it does not claim that
local historical fixtures were available. Quick Mapper retains its own validator.
