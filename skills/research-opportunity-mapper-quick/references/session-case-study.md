## Question and evidence

A user asks whether an observed improvement identifies a useful research direction.
Separate the reported observation from the proposed mechanism. Identify what each
source measured, its comparator, its limitations, and whether apparent replications
share a source. More rows or deeper reading do not create independent evidence.

## Selection and validation

Explain the proposed route in prose. Compare its strongest alternative explanation
under a fair baseline. Distinguish an experimental stage or a comparator from an
independent research route. Follow this Skill's own run mode and schema; this example
does not override their contracts or prescribe a candidate count.

## Interpretation

State the positive, negative, and ambiguous outcomes before work begins. Unknown
capabilities remain unknown until verified. Explain which result would change the
decision and which claims would remain unsupported. Keep reader-facing judgments
consistent with the authoritative work artifacts.
